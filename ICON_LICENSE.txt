Parts Manager icon

The bundled icon is an original simple automotive illustration created for the Parts Manager installer.
It is not a brand logo and does not use a vehicle manufacturer trademark.
It was designed after reviewing public-domain / CC0 automotive icon references, including: 
https://www.svgrepo.com/svg/7472963/auto-parts

The referenced SVG Repo item is marked CC0 on the source page.
The bundled Parts Manager icon itself is original artwork and may be used, modified, and distributed with the Parts Manager product.
